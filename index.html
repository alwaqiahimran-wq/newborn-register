More registration help →
Will my baby receive a NIN immediately?

No. Birth details, parent information and supporting records must be reviewed first. NIRA is responsible for national identification; this portal does not itself allocate a NIN or guarantee a processing time.

What if one parent does not have an ID?

Use the first-time parent route. A registration officer can explain the documents and verification needed for your circumstances. Do not enter someone else’s NIN or make up an ID number.

Why is LC residence confirmation requested?

It can support verification of the family’s stated residence. The officer should confirm the required format and verify it with the relevant local authority. A chairman’s photograph is not a substitute for that verification.

Can a guardian register a child?

Ask the hospital registration desk about your situation. Guardianship or other supporting records may be needed, especially when a parent is unavailable.
