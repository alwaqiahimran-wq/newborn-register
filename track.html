What each stage means.

A guide to the registration journey, not a live status. Your officer can confirm which checks apply.

  1. 01

    Application received

    The birth details and supporting records have been submitted for review. Receipt is not approval.

  2. 02

    Parent verification

    Parent or guardian details are checked. First-time national ID applicants may need separate enrolment.

  3. 03

    Residence review

    Residence details and any requested LC confirmation are reviewed with the relevant local authority.

  4. 04

    NIN processing

    Verified details proceed through the authorised process. Only the relevant authority can confirm NIN allocation.

If more information is needed: confirm exactly what is missing with the registration officer before supplying another document. A review stage does not guarantee a completion date.

Waiting for an update?

Here’s what to do in the meantime.

What if a parent’s first national ID is still pending?

A parent’s ID enrolment and the newborn’s registration are separate processes. Tell the hospital registration officer that the parent’s ID is pending and ask which verification steps remain. See first-time parent guidance.

How do I correct a detail or replace an unclear image?

Contact the hospital registration desk with your application reference. Ask how to correct the record or securely provide the requested image. Do not create a duplicate application or send ID photographs through unverified channels.

Does LC confirmation mean the application is approved?

No. A signed residence confirmation can support a residence check, but it does not confirm parent identity or approve a NIN. An LC chairman’s photograph alone is not proof of residence.

Why can’t I see my actual application here?

This prototype is not connected to hospital systems or NIRA. It cannot check whether a reference exists, show live progress or issue a NIN. Ask the hospital where you submitted your registration for a verified update.
