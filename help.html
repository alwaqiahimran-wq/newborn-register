Parents & identity

What if one or both parents have no national ID?

If a parent has never registered for a national ID, use the first-time ID guidance. Tell the registration officer which parent has no ID so they can explain the separate enrolment and identity verification steps.

Do not invent a NIN or use another person’s number. Read guidance for first-time parent ID applicants.

A parent is unavailable. Can a guardian help?

Ask the hospital registration desk before submitting. Explain who is caring for the child and which parent’s details are unavailable. The officer can advise on consent, guardianship records and any other supporting evidence needed for your circumstances.

The names on our documents do not match. What should we do?

Do not change details simply to make the documents match. Bring the available records to the registration desk and explain the difference. An officer should advise whether a correction or additional evidence is needed before verification can proceed.

Images & residence

How do I prepare a readable document image?
  • Place the document flat in good light, without glare or shadows.
  • Include all four corners and keep names, dates and document numbers readable.
  • Use an original, unfiltered image. Do not edit the information on it.

The homepage’s local preview accepts JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 5 MB each. It does not upload or submit them.

Why won’t my image preview open?

Check that the file is JPG, PNG or WebP and no larger than 5 MB. HEIC images and PDF documents are not supported by the homepage preview. Export a supported copy or take a new photograph without losing readable detail.

If a supported file still fails, try another image. A successful preview only checks display; it does not mean the document has been verified.

What is needed from the LC chairman?

Ask the registration officer whether LC residence confirmation is required and what format to use. An image of a signed residence confirmation may be requested to support the family’s stated address.

The LC chairman’s image is the final supporting image in the preview sequence. Only provide it if requested and with the chairman’s consent. A photograph alone does not prove residence; the officer must verify the confirmation with the relevant local authority.

Does selecting an image mean I have submitted it?

No. On this prototype, selected images are previewed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and no registration application is created by the homepage preview. Use “Clear selected images” to remove previews.

Use sample images until an authorised hospital service is connected. Never send ID images or a child’s records through an unverified contact.

Applications & NINs

How do I check an application’s progress?

Go to Track an application. For an application submitted through an authorised service, keep the reference or acknowledgement provided to you.

This prototype cannot retrieve live hospital or government records. If you do not have a reference, ask the hospital where you registered rather than starting a duplicate application.

Can I correct details after submitting?

For a real submission, contact the hospital registration desk with your application reference, if available. Explain which detail is incorrect and bring the supporting record. Ask for the appropriate correction process; do not submit a second application unless an officer advises you to.

When will my baby receive a NIN?

A NIN is not issued immediately by this portal. The birth record, parents’ identities and supporting information must be reviewed. NIRA is responsible for national identification and allocation through its authorised process.

No processing time is guaranteed here. Ask the registration desk which stage your application has reached and whether anything else is needed.

Is a hospital birth record the same as a birth certificate?

No. A hospital birth notification or delivery record documents the birth at the facility. It is not a national ID, a NIN or a birth certificate. Ask the hospital registration desk about the separate civil registration steps that apply to your child.
